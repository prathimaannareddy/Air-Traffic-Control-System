# US Air Traffic Delay Patterns — Visual Dashboard

College project for a graph-visualization course: an interactive Plotly Dash
dashboard exploring 2015 US flight delays, with a geographic map, a network-graph
view of the national route network (centrality + Louvain communities), time
patterns, and airline/cause comparisons.

Dataset: Kaggle "2015 Flight Delays and Cancellations" (~5.8M rows).
Raw CSVs live in `data/raw/` (`flights.csv`, `airports.csv`, `airlines.csv`).

## Features

- **KPI cards** — total flights, % delayed (arrival delay > 15 min), average
  arrival delay, worst airport, busiest hub — all recomputed under filters.
- **Sidebar filters** — airline (multi-select), month range slider, origin
  airport, delay cause, top-N routes on map. Everything updates the charts;
  empty results show a friendly placeholder.
- **Overview tab** — key insights (worst airport, worst hour, top cause, most
  central hub, community count), monthly trend, top-10 worst airports & routes
  (min-flight threshold to avoid noise).
- **Map & Network tab** — scattergeo of US airports (size = traffic, color =
  avg delay) with top-N route lines; network graph of the top-60 airports
  (nodes on geographic positions, sized by traffic, colored by Louvain
  community or delay); centrality ranking bar (betweenness / PageRank / degree).
- **Time Patterns tab** — hour × day-of-week heatmap of average delay + monthly
  trend (avg delay and delay rate), both rebuilt from filtered data.
- **Airlines & Causes tab** — airline comparison (avg delay + delay rate, dual
  axis) and stacked delay-cause bars per airline and per month
  (air system / security / airline / late aircraft / weather).
- **Graph Insights tab** — betweenness vs delay bubble chart + explainer panel.

## Data notes & assumptions

- Delayed flight = `ARRIVAL_DELAY > 15` minutes.
- Cancelled and diverted flights removed; rows with missing arrival delay removed.
- Numeric airport codes absent from `airports.csv` dropped; airports without
  lat/lon excluded from the map/network.
- Delay-cause charts respond to the airline and month filters (cause minutes are
  not recorded per origin airport in the source data).
- The graph/network/centrality views use the full-year network (Stage 2 output).
- "Worst" rankings require a minimum flight count (500 full-year; scaled down
  adaptively under filters) to avoid tiny-sample noise.
- Betweenness uses k=200 sampled sources for speed.
